## The taste test

A line needs a mechanism. A speaker confidently disproving their own point is stronger than a speaker merely saying something strange. A precise insult beats generic profanity. A short, unnecessary qualification can be the whole joke. Official prose is funny when its priorities expose something absurd.

Read the line without the name. If it is only amusing after several sentences of explanation, it should not open a game. Then read all four choices. The wrong answers need to be believable and entertaining; three arbitrary celebrities are not misdirection. The actual source should either be a surprise or make an already funny line better.

This is not a ban on profanity, darkness or food. The grandmother overruling 50 Cent's wealth is funny because his status instantly collapses. The floating turd has no comparable structure. The former remains a headliner; the latter is reserve. Likewise, the chicken, scatology, bodily-function and random-animal novelties have largely moved to the back.

Exact wording matters. Satie uses the translation in the cited lecture. Austen's letter is dated 24 December, not 18 December. Nanni is the copper customer; Ea-nasir is the seller. Martha Stewart's excerpt is continuous and is identified as a joke about married crushes. Pattinson's proposal is explicitly hypothetical. The GLaDOS source includes the original game audio. Familiar quotations without secure attribution were not added merely because they circulated widely.

This pass researched all new additions and spot-checked promoted older material; it is not a fresh historical audit of all 714 records. Existing low-confidence research remains held out by the builder.

## Selection behaviour

New material is not a fixed first-game script. Every game shuffles within priority tiers, begins with up to three unseen openers from different topics, and prefers varied speakers and topics. It spends a stronger tier before weakening the selection merely to satisfy those preferences.

Unseen headliners come before unseen featured, ordinary and reserve material. Every unseen quote still precedes repeats. A returning player's saved history therefore remains useful, and newly added headliners naturally rise to the front. Completing the whole collection resets the seen set as before.

Regression checks cover topic and speaker pressure, reserve ordering, discovery with existing history, five fresh curated games and a complete collection without repeats. Browser checks cover a full game, saved history, keyboard/touch interaction and several screen sizes.
